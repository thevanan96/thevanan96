# About Me 👋

I'm **Thevanankumaran Jegakumaran (Thev)**, founder & CEO of **JTK LABS (PVT) LTD**, a Sri Lanka-based service and product based software company. We build our own products and also partner with clients around the world to design, build and ship software.

We specialize in **AI automation**, helping businesses save time and cost by putting AI to work on their everyday processes.

### 🛠️ Our Services

- 🤖 **AI Automation** (our specialty): automating business workflows and repetitive tasks with AI
- 🧠 **Agentic AI:** building AI agents that reason, use tools and complete tasks autonomously
- 🌍 **Web Development:** modern, scalable web applications and SaaS platforms
- 📱 **Mobile App Development:** iOS and Android apps for healthcare, veterinary and other industries
